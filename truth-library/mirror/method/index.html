How the Administrative Mirror works

Section 1

What the Administrative Mirror Is

The Administrative Mirror is a standardized way of comparing what laws require, what agencies say, what public records show, and what courts can and cannot compel.

Each page follows the same four-part structure so differences and repetitions are visible without interpretation.

The format is intentionally constrained. If a fact cannot be placed cleanly into one of the four boxes, it does not go on the page.

Section 2

What the Mirror Is Not

  • Not an advocacy campaign
  • Not enforcement
  • Not naming individuals
  • Not assigning intent or motive
  • Not scoring or ranking agencies
  • Not legal advice

The Mirror documents public information. It does not argue for outcomes.

Section 3

How a Mirror Page Is Built

Every Mirror page uses the same four-box structure:

1. Statute Required

What the law says the agency must do, with citation.

2. Agency Said

What the agency publicly stated about the duty.

3. Record Shows

What public records document actually happened.

4. Courts Can / Cannot

What judicial remedies exist and their limits.

The constraint is the product. If a fact cannot be placed cleanly into one box, it does not appear on the page. This prevents interpretation from entering the structure.
Section 4

How the Mirror Updates Over Time

Mirror pages are designed for updates as agency actions change:

  • Pages are versioned with dates
  • Sources are checked against current public records
  • When agencies act, pages are updated to reflect new status
  • Previous versions are preserved for reference

The system is designed for maintenance, not one-time publication.

Section 5

Who Uses This and Why

  • Researchers — cross-agency comparison using consistent structure
  • Journalists — primary sources and official citations in one place
  • Educators — systems literacy through concrete examples
  • Public — verification without needing legal expertise

The Mirror makes agency accountability visible. What people do with that visibility is their choice.
