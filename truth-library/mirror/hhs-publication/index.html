HHS — Statutory Publication Duties

Administrative Mirror page comparing statutory duties, agency statements, administrative record facts, and judicial remedy limits. No individuals are named. No conclusions are asserted.

① What the Statute Required

  • Law
    Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) § 110(g) and Affordable Care Act § 3011 (42 U.S.C. § 280j)
  • Subject
    Mandatory publication of food safety research plans and national health quality strategy updates
  • Responsible agency
    Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), including FDA and AHRQ
  • Statutory duties
    (1) FSMA § 110(g): Publish biennial Food Safety and Food Defense Research Plan; (2) ACA § 3011: Submit and publish annual updates to the National Health Care Quality Strategy
  • Deadline
    Biennial (FSMA § 110); Annual (ACA § 3011)
  • Nature of duty
    Discrete · Mandatory · Recurring
These statutes require publication to the public, not internal consideration or discretionary review.

② What the Agency Said

HHS agencies have publicly stated they are:

  • Coordinating food safety research across HHS, USDA, and DHS
  • Publishing related reports through AHRQ's National Healthcare Quality and Disparities Report
  • Prioritizing food safety and quality improvement initiatives
Public statements reference ongoing activities, not completion of specific statutory publication duties.

③ What the Administrative Record Shows

  • May 2015: FDA/HHS published "Biennial Report to Congress on the Food Safety and Food Defense Research Plan" explicitly citing FSMA § 110(g)
  • 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023 cycles: No subsequent biennial publications titled as § 110(g) research plans located on FDA's public FSMA "Reports & Studies" repository
  • Related reports: NAFDS reports issued under FSMA § 108 (2015, 2019) capture some food defense research, but do not carry the § 110(g) research plan title
  • National Quality Strategy: Initial strategy published 2011; annual standalone updates not consistently published as required by ACA § 3011
  • As of filing: A § 110(g)-titled biennial research plan has not been publicly located beyond the 2015 publication
The required publications — as specified by statute — have not been maintained on the mandated schedule.

④ What Courts Can / Cannot Compel

Under the Administrative Procedure Act (APA):

Courts can:

  • Declare agency action unlawfully withheld
  • Order publication of required reports by a court-set deadline

Courts cannot:

  • Dictate the content of required reports
  • Direct research priorities or policy conclusions
  • Supervise ongoing agency operations beyond ordering publication
Judicial remedies focus on compelling publication, not directing content.
