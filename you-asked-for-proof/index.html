1. Retirement Risk Was Shifted to Workers

For decades, many workers were promised retirement through employer pensions. Over time: pensions declined, individual retirement accounts replaced them, and workers took on market risk. After major market crashes in 2000 and 2008: retirement accounts lost trillions, employers froze or closed pension plans, and those plans were rarely restored. This shift remained in place.

Proof:

  • GAO report on pension freezes and plan terminations
  • SSA analysis of disappearing pensions
  • CRS summary of continued decline
